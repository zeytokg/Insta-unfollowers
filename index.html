Instagram Unfollowers Finder

by zeytokg

Scans the accounts you follow and shows you who doesn't follow you back. Everything runs in your own browser, using your own session — no data is ever sent anywhere else.

How to use it

1

Log into instagram.com.

2

Open your browser's developer console — Ctrl+Shift+J on Windows/Linux, Cmd+Option+J on macOS.

3

Copy the code below, paste it into the console, and press Enter. If Chrome blocks pasting, type allow pasting, press Enter, then paste again.

4

Click "Scan now" in the panel that appears in the top-right corner.

What it does

  • Only scans the accounts you follow (not your entire follower list).
  • Uses Instagram's own follow-back status to find who doesn't follow you back.
  • Includes search, filters (verified / private), hide, and copy.
  • Lets you unfollow a selection slowly and safely, with pause/cancel controls.
  • Nothing on your account changes during the scan itself.
